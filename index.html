How It Works

From your goals to your plate — in three steps

01

Set Your Goals

Tell MealMuse your dietary preferences, fitness targets, and lifestyle. It builds a personalised baseline around you — not a generic template.

02

Log & Track

Log meals with natural language, track workouts with progressive overload guidance, and manage recipes — all in one place, with no manual macro math.

03

Get AI Recommendations

Claude analyses your history and goals to suggest meals, adjust your plan, and generate a grocery list — context-aware, not cookie-cutter.

Features

Core capabilities built with modern iOS frameworks and cloud infrastructure

AI Meal Recommendations

Get meal suggestions that fit your macros, preferences, and schedule — powered by Claude, not a static algorithm.

Workout Tracking

Log your sets, reps, and weight. MealMuse knows when to push you harder and when to hold back — progressive overload built in.

Smart Meal Logging

Just type what you ate. MealMuse parses the macros automatically — no barcode scanning, no manual entry, no friction.

Personalized to You

The more you use it, the better it gets. MealMuse adapts its suggestions to your habits, goals, and history over time.

Recipe Book

Save, organise, and revisit your favourite meals. Your recipe library syncs across sessions and feeds back into your AI recommendations.

Grocery Lists

MealMuse turns your weekly meal plan into a ready-to-shop grocery list — automatically grouped and ready to go.

Always in Sync

Your data lives in the cloud. Pick up on any device, right where you left off — no manual backups, no data loss.

Engineering & Architecture

Technical decisions made for scalability, performance, and production readiness

SwiftUI Firebase Auth Firestore Render Claude API Combine MVVM Node.js

Frontend & AI Integration

Declarative SwiftUI with reactive state via Combine. AI responses from the Claude API are streamed through a Node.js orchestration layer, parsed into structured Swift models, and rendered with zero UI blocking.

SwiftUI MVVM Combine Claude API Prompt Engineering

Backend & Infrastructure

Firebase Authentication and Firestore for real-time data with offline persistence. Render-hosted Node.js services for scalable AI orchestration.

Firebase Firestore Render Serverless CI/CD

Data Architecture

Document-based data model with reactive sync. Environment-based configuration management for production deployment pipeline.

NoSQL Real-time Offline-First Scalable

AI/LLM Workflows

Claude handles multi-turn conversation context, structured output generation, and schema-constrained meal/macro extraction. Prompt templates are versioned and token-budgeted to keep inference costs predictable at scale.

Token Optimization Context Windows Structured Output
