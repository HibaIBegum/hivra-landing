Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 12, 2026  ·  App: HivRa  ·  Developer: Hiba Begum

HivRa does not sell your data or use it for advertising. All data collected is used solely to provide the app's features - personalized meal recommendations, workout tracking, grocery list sharing, and AI-assisted suggestions.

Data We Collect

HivRa collects the following information to provide its features:

  • Account Credentials - Email address and password, stored securely via Firebase Authentication. Your email is used for login and grocery list sharing only.
  • Profile Data - Age, height, weight, sex, and fitness goals.
  • Health & Fitness Data - Steps, active calories, and heart rate read from Apple HealthKit; workout logs and nutrition entries you create in the app.
  • User Content - Meal plans, recipes, grocery lists, and workout records you create.
  • Usage Data - Streak activity and app interaction patterns.

How We Use Your Data

All data is used solely to provide HivRa's features - personalized meal recommendations, workout tracking, grocery list sharing, and AI-assisted suggestions. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

AI Recommendations

Meal and nutrition recommendations are generated by sending anonymized goal and nutrition context to a third-party AI API. No personally identifiable information is transmitted to the AI service.

HealthKit

HivRa reads steps, active calories, and heart rate from Apple HealthKit to improve workout and meal recommendations. HealthKit data is never shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Data Sharing

Your email address is visible to other HivRa users only when you share a grocery list with them. No other personal data is shared with other users.

Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Google Firestore and Firebase Authentication infrastructure.

Account Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
